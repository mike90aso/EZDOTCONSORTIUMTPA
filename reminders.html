Driver Reminders

Text and email reminders

Opt in if you want EZ DOT Consortium TPA to contact you about your DOT Physical expiration, discounted DOT Physicals, and other cost-saving services.

What the messages cover

Texts and emails go only to drivers who submit this form and check the consent box.

DOT Physical expiration

We use the expiration date you give us, or the date already on file, and send a reminder before that certificate runs out.

Discounted DOT Physicals

When a reduced exam price is available, we can send that offer by text and email.

Other services and advertisements

Occasional messages about other cost-saving services. These are advertisements, not required safety notices.

Random drug and alcohol tests are not included. A random selection is unannounced. This list will not text or email you that a random test is coming up.

Name, email, and mobile number

Use the phone that can receive text messages. You can opt out later by replying STOP to a text or asking us by email.
