Motor carriers

CDL drivers enrolled in a managed random pool, with records kept for audit.

Owner-operators

Single-driver employers are required to use a C/TPA for random selections.

Trucking schools

Pre-employment testing and program coordination for drivers placed in service.

DOT testing services

Scheduled at a collection site, reviewed by a laboratory and a Medical Review Officer, and filed with the program record.

Pre-Employment Drug Testing

Before a driver performs safety-sensitive work, paired with the pre-employment Clearinghouse query.

Random Drug and Alcohol Testing

Drivers are placed in a combined pool. Draws run through the year at the FMCSA annual random rates.

Post-Accident Drug and Alcohol Testing

Arranged after a qualifying accident, inside the time windows the regulations set.

Reasonable Suspicion Testing

When a trained supervisor documents observable signs, we coordinate the drug test, the alcohol test, or both.

Return-to-Duty Testing

Completed after a substance abuse professional determines the driver is eligible to test, and before safety-sensitive work resumes.

Follow-Up Drug and Alcohol Testing

The tests required by the SAP’s follow-up plan after the driver returns to work. Dates are not set with the driver in advance.

Return-to-duty services

Referral, testing, and Clearinghouse reporting after a drug or alcohol violation, when you designate us to perform that work.

SAP Referral & Coordination

Referral to a qualified substance abuse professional, from the initial assessment through eligibility for return-to-duty testing.

Return-to-Duty Testing

The drug test, alcohol test, or both, scheduled once the SAP determines the driver may test.

Follow-Up Testing Administration

The SAP’s written plan administered in full: test count, time period, and a record of each result.

Unannounced Follow-Up Testing

Follow-up tests are not scheduled on a date the driver chooses. Notice is issued when the test is due.

Clearinghouse RTD Reporting

Negative return-to-duty results and completion of the follow-up plan, reported in the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse.

Program administration

The ongoing work that keeps a DOT testing program in order.

Random pool administration

Roster maintenance, selections, and notice to the company when a driver is selected.

Clearinghouse queries

Pre-employment and annual queries, with driver consent, when you authorize the query.

Supervisor training

Reasonable-suspicion training for supervisors responsible for observing and documenting signs.

Records and MIS reporting

Testing records retained for the required periods and compiled for audit or a Management Information System report.

The employer remains responsible for compliance with 49 CFR Part 40 and Part 382. Services are limited to those named in a written agreement. Owner-operators must designate a C/TPA in the FMCSA Clearinghouse.
