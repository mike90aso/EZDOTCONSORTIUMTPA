Providers needed

EZ DOT Consortium TPA is adding DOT Physical examiners and drug and alcohol collection sites as the program expands across the United States.

Apply to join
  • DOT Physical providersFMCSA National Registry medical examiners who can see referred drivers.
  • Drug and alcohol providersCollection sites that can perform DOT random drug tests and alcohol tests.

Who we are adding

Both roles support carriers, owner-operators, and trucking schools already in the consortium.

DOT Physical providers

Certified medical examiners listed on the FMCSA National Registry. Exams are for drivers referred by the consortium. You set the appointment; we send the driver.

Random drug and alcohol testing providers

Sites that can collect a DOT urine drug test and conduct a breath alcohol test when a driver is selected from the random pool. Random tests are unannounced, so the site needs to take a driver on short notice during business hours.

What we ask of a site

A clinic may apply for one role or both.

For Physical exams

Current National Registry certification, a published clinic address, and the ability to schedule a DOT medical exam for a referred driver.

For random testing

Qualified DOT collectors, evidential breath alcohol testing, and a process for specimens that meets 49 CFR Part 40. The site should be able to accept a randomly selected driver without a long booking window.

Coverage

We are filling gaps in the national network. Tell us the city and state, and whether you can cover nearby areas.

How referrals work

Drivers are sent to a provider near them. You remain the clinic of record for the exam or the collection. We coordinate the referral.
